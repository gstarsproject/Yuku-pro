# Security and launch checklist

## Product safety
- Friend category is for lawful social companionship, not sexual services, escorting, exploitation, or illegal activity.
- Public meeting guidance and reporting flow for in-person bookings.
- Emergency/help escalation and trusted-contact sharing are planned features.
- Age eligibility and guardian restrictions must be defined with Indonesian legal advice before launch.
- High-risk professional categories require credential checks and category-specific safeguards.

## Privacy
- Collect only necessary identity and location data.
- Ask location permission only when needed; allow manual city selection.
- Do not show exact home address publicly.
- Encrypt sensitive documents at rest and restrict staff access.
- Define retention, deletion, consent, and data-subject request workflows.
- Prepare privacy notice and terms in Bahasa Indonesia.

## Financial controls
- Use a licensed/appropriate payment service provider.
- Do not store card numbers or CVV.
- Verify signed webhooks server-side.
- Ledger is append-only; adjustments are reversal entries, not edits.
- Separate gross amount, platform fee, provider payable, tax, refunds, and gateway fees.
- Reconcile payouts and gateway settlement daily.
- Obtain legal/accounting advice on marketplace funds handling and tax obligations.

## Operations
- Admin MFA and role-based access.
- Audit all sensitive admin actions.
- Rate limits, abuse detection, spam prevention.
- Backups and restore drills.
- Monitoring, incident response, support SLA.
- Security testing before production.

## Indonesia launch review
Obtain qualified advice on applicable Indonesian requirements including personal data protection, electronic system operation, consumer protection, tax, payment services, labor/contractor classification, and professional licensing. Do not launch regulated professional categories until their requirements are verified.
