# YUKU Brand Guideline v1.0

## Brand
- Name: YUKU
- Slogan: Yuk, Ketemu.
- Category: Social & Professional Services Marketplace
- Promise: Helping people find trusted people and book useful services with clarity and confidence.
- Primary market: Indonesia
- Language at launch: Bahasa Indonesia

## Positioning
YUKU brings three service pillars into one marketplace:
1. YUKU Friend — paid social companionship for lawful activities such as conversation, meals, movies, study, sports, and public outings.
2. YUKU Experience — local guides, trip planning, travel companions, photography experiences, and activities.
3. YUKU Pro — skilled professional services such as tutoring, design, translation, photography, cleaning, and eligible care services.

## Personality
Warm, trustworthy, modern, inclusive, helpful, and professional. Avoid dating-app implications and avoid language suggesting sexual services.

## Visual system
- Deep Navy: `#101A39` — primary brand surface and premium headers
- Electric Blue: `#286BFF` — primary actions and active states
- Violet: `#8057E8` — secondary accent
- Soft White: `#F5F7FC` — page background
- Ink: `#172033` — primary text
- Muted: `#697586` — secondary text
- Success: `#16865B`
- Warning: `#C47A13`
- Danger: `#C43D4B`

## Typography
Use Inter or a visually equivalent sans-serif. Use clear hierarchy:
- Display: 30–36 px, semibold/bold
- H1: 24–28 px, semibold
- H2: 20–22 px, semibold
- Body: 14–16 px, regular
- Caption: 12–13 px

## UI principles
- White/soft-white content surfaces with navy anchors.
- Electric blue for the main CTA; one dominant CTA per screen.
- Rounded cards (12–18 px), restrained shadows, generous whitespace.
- Real provider photos; no fake review/rating claims.
- Always disclose total price, duration, cancellation terms, and whether travel costs are included.
- Show provider verification level accurately; never imply government endorsement unless formally granted.
- Accessible contrast, scalable text, clear focus and error states.

## Voice and tone
Friendly and concise. Examples:
- “Cari layanan”
- “Lihat profil”
- “Pilih jadwal”
- “Ajukan booking”
- “Menunggu konfirmasi”
- “Booking selesai”

## Naming
Use “Provider” for service providers, “Customer” for customers, “Booking” for an order, and “Layanan” for a service. Avoid using “host” in the core marketplace experience.
